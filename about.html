Chairman's message

Welcome to SIG Construction & Furnishing PLC

Our journey is driven by a simple vision: to create exceptional spaces and infrastructure that improve lives, strengthen businesses, and contribute to national development.

As Ethiopia continues its remarkable transformation, the demand for world-class construction, interior solutions, and premium building materials continues to grow. SIG Construction & Furnishing PLC was established to meet this demand through innovation, professionalism, and uncompromising quality.

Every project we undertake reflects our commitment to craftsmanship, safety and long-term partnership with the clients and communities we serve.
